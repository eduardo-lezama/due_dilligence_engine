## FILINGS REPORT (10-K)
Business: Build-A-Bear Workshop, Inc. is a specialty retailer that allows customers, primarily children and families, to create personalized stuffed animals through an interactive experience. The company operates a unique retail model that combines entertainment and retail, offering both in-store experiences and e-commerce options. Build-A-Bear generates revenue through direct-to-consumer sales, commercial transactions, and international franchising, leveraging its brand strength and pop-culture appeal to attract a diverse customer base.
Key risks: The company faces several material risks including macroeconomic uncertainties that could reduce discretionary consumer spending, competitive pressures from both traditional retailers and online platforms, and the impact of inflation and tariffs on costs and profitability. Additionally, the reliance on shopping malls and tourist locations for foot traffic poses a risk to sales, particularly in light of changing consumer behaviors and economic conditions.
SPECIFIC REGULATORY EVENTS: On February 20, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court issued a ruling striking down certain tariffs previously imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act ("IEEPA"). Following this decision, the U.S. presidential administration announced its intention to invoke other laws to collect tariffs and announced new tariffs on imports from all countries, in addition to any existing non-IEEPA tariffs.
MDA highlights (LLM summary): Management highlights that fiscal 2025 was marked by a significant increase in net retail sales, driven by growth in corporately-operated locations and a successful omnichannel strategy. The company reported a pre-tax profit of $67.2 million, the highest in its history, and ended the year with no borrowings under its credit agreement. However, challenges such as rising labor costs and inflationary pressures were noted as ongoing concerns that could impact future profitability.
