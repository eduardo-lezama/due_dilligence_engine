## FILINGS REPORT (10-K)
Business: AutoZone, Inc. is a leading retailer and distributor of automotive replacement parts and accessories in the Americas. The company operates 6,627 stores in the U.S., 883 in Mexico, and 147 in Brazil, selling a wide range of products for cars, SUVs, vans, and light-duty trucks. Revenue is generated primarily through the sale of automotive hard parts, maintenance items, accessories, and non-automotive products, with a significant portion coming from commercial sales to repair garages and service stations.
Key risks: Key risks include regulatory uncertainties stemming from global trade tariffs and geopolitical tensions, competitive pressures from other retailers with greater resources, and technological advancements that may reduce demand for traditional automotive parts. Additionally, economic factors such as inflation and consumer spending habits pose risks to sales.
MDA highlights: For fiscal 2025, AutoZone reported net sales of $18.9 billion, a 2.4% increase from the previous year, with domestic commercial sales rising by 6.7%. However, operating profit and net income decreased due to unfavorable foreign currency exchange rates and increased costs. The company continues to focus on the aging vehicle population and miles driven as key drivers of demand.
