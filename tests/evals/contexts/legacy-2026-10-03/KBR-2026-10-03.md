## FILINGS REPORT (10-K)
Business: KBR, Inc. delivers science, technology, engineering, and logistics support solutions primarily to the U.S. federal government, allied nations, and commercial clients. The company focuses on national security, defense systems engineering, sustainable energy, and advanced digital solutions. KBR's business model leverages its deep domain expertise and full-life cycle capabilities to address complex challenges faced by its clients.
Key risks: KBR faces significant risks related to its dependence on large contracts with government entities, particularly the U.S. government, which accounted for 57% of its revenues in fiscal 2025. Budget uncertainties, potential government shutdowns, and acquisition reforms pose threats to its financial performance. Additionally, geopolitical conditions and trade restrictions could adversely impact operations and profitability.
SPECIFIC REGULATORY EVENTS: January 20, 2025: Executive order signed to create an advisory commission, DOGE, to reform federal government processes and reduce expenditures. April 15, 2025: Executive Order 14275 signed, directing revisions to the FAR for more efficient procurement. November 7, 2025: U.S. DoW released a memorandum on defense acquisition reform.
MDA highlights: Management highlights challenges such as delays in project execution and contract awards due to government shutdowns and budgetary uncertainties. The company anticipates continued opportunities in defense spending, driven by geopolitical factors and the need for technology advancements. KBR is also monitoring macroeconomic conditions that could affect its operations and client investments in energy transition solutions.
