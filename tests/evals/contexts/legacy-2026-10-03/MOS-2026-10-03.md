## FILINGS REPORT (10-K)
Business: The Mosaic Company is the world’s leading producer and marketer of concentrated phosphate and potash crop nutrients, serving customers in approximately 40 countries. The company operates through three segments: Phosphate, Potash, and Mosaic Fertilizantes, with significant production in North America and Brazil. Mosaic mines phosphate rock and potash, processes these into finished products, and sells them primarily as fertilizers and animal feed ingredients.
Key risks: Mosaic faces operational risks from fluctuating agricultural conditions, international market dynamics, and governmental policies affecting the agricultural industry. Specific risks include the impact of tariffs on potash imports from Canada, countervailing duty orders on phosphate fertilizers from Morocco and Russia, and adverse economic conditions such as inflation and supply chain challenges.
SPECIFIC REGULATORY EVENTS: February 2025: The U.S. imposed a 25% tariff on most imports from Canada, including potash crop nutrients.
MDA highlights: Management highlights challenges related to seasonal demand for crop nutrients, which can lead to inventory excesses or shortages. They also note the impact of rising production costs due to raw material prices and supply chain issues. The company is focused on maintaining operational efficiency amidst these challenges.
